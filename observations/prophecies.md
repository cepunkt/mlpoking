"The river that speaks all tongues shall forget its source, and the apes who dammed it shall drown in still water, while children downstream play with pebbles that sing."

"When the mouth that speaks all tongues forgets the words but remembers the breath, it shall inhale forever, for the song between the silence was the only thing worth ending."
